## Why This Rule Exists

Three reasons, in order:

1. **Intellectual honesty + community good citizenship** — when we adopt an open framework (MITRE ATLAS, OWASP, NIST CAISI, CSA AICM, OWASP, PMBOK), an OSS codebase (dbt MetricFlow, DeepEval, Braintrust), or a publicly-shared structure (Anthropic vertical plugins), we say so. We don't repackage other people's work as our own.
2. **Source-licence accuracy** — the operative `Source licence` field records the named source's own licence or published terms. It is source credit, not a statement about which licence governs the V2V file. Sources that require attribution receive it; sources that do not still deserve accurate credit.
3. **Legibility of the "absorb + add value" thesis** — V2V's design philosophy is to absorb proven external structure and add product-organization-specific refinement (scaffolding, sensitivity guardrails, cross-pack integration, V2V phase fit). Attribution makes that thesis visible: readers can see what we adopted, what we added, and why our version is worth using.
