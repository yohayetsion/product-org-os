### Worked Example

A sensitive skill produces a structured first-pass extraction of 25 resumes for a recruiter to review. The skill takes 90 seconds of wall clock and the recruiter would otherwise have spent ~4 hours producing the same structured extraction manually before substantive review begins.

- **Prohibited ROI line**: "~4 hrs of HR review saved in 90s" — implies the skill performed the review.
- **Prohibited ROI line**: "~4 hrs of recruiter time saved on candidate review" — same issue, indirect via "candidate review."
- **Acceptable ROI line**: "~4 hrs saved on drafting and triage of structured resume extraction in 90s" — accurately frames the skill as drafting + triage, leaves review time as the recruiter's required work.
- **Acceptable ROI line**: "~4 hrs saved producing structured first-pass extraction input for recruiter review in 90s" — explicit about the input-to-review framing.

Both acceptable forms preserve the principle that the recruiter still owns review and that review time is unchanged and required.
