### Relationship to Other Patterns

Pattern 5 is NOT Pattern 4 Debate. Debate requires both sides to have independent merit and the outcome to be genuinely uncertain. Adversarial Review assumes the drafter has a position and we are stress-testing it — the adversarial agent is not advocating for a different deliverable, it is finding weaknesses in the current one.

Pattern 5 is stronger than Pattern 3 Review because of the role separation and fresh-context requirement. Use Review for routine quality validation and Adversarial Review when the cost of a missed issue is material.
